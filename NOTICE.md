# Notice

Orchestra is maintained by Vlad Kalashnikov.

Its selective-routing contracts, custom-agent profile design, safe installer,
and runtime inspector are adapted from Daniel McAteer's
[Sol Advisor](https://github.com/DannyMac180/sol-advisor) v0.6.0 at commit
`37b75cad535abdd46531f0227483a8842d045ab8`, distributed under the MIT License.

Orchestra uses its own namespace and preserves the pinned role model: Sol / High
manages, Luna / Max handles bounded work, Terra / High handles judgment-heavy work,
and a fresh read-only Sol / High performs optional review. Orchestra's strategy-first
routing, Context Packets, modifiers, and run metadata are project-specific additions.
